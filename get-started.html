Never opened a terminal before? Here's how

On Windows:

  1. Press the Start menu, type PowerShell, press Enter.
  2. A black or blue window opens — that's your terminal.
  3. Right-click inside it to paste the copied command, then press Enter.

On Mac:

  1. Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  2. A window opens — that's your terminal.
  3. Press Cmd + V to paste the copied command, then press Enter.
What actually happens when I press Enter?

The first time you run a model, your computer downloads it — that can take a few minutes depending on your internet and the model's size. After that, it starts a chat right there in the terminal. Type a message and press Enter to talk to it. Every time after the first, it starts instantly since it's already downloaded.
